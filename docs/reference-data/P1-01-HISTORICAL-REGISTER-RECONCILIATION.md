## 1. Source hierarchy and legally distinct layers

- **Decree No. 85-20 (2 February 1985):** creates the territorial register and defines the four-digit commune-code structure and transfer rules. Article 3 says the first two digits identify the Wilaya and the last two the commune; Article 4 requires a new code when a commune is transferred to another Wilaya and does not reallocate an old code. Official PDFs: `F1985006.PDF` (`402c46c62af42a222f997506cc32452dde08611baacf9a9437de7be037d60659`) and `A1985006.PDF` (`afadc45a4acb13b0816bfcafdf713f71038c6b6f2f1d07a93fe0454c6b798bcc`).
- **Interministerial order 22 June 1985, JO 44, published 23 October 1985:** initial 48-Wilaya commune/code/seat register (`F1985044.PDF`, `bc9f42cbf3d7bb1fc576383ea8d7194d6ae7a06945003b5ede31581e9a8daf2`; `A1985044.PDF`, `a90f0306f236fea45956377b8eb5944c07f7e3b995db971edaf35fd6bff4bc59`). Key disputed sections (Boumerdès, El Tarf, M'Sila, El Oued) are now visually PNG-verified in both French (printed pp. 1065–1070) and Arabic (printed pp. 1631–1635); full 975-row extraction across the 30 remaining unmodified Wilayas still requires national concordance assembly.
- **Interministerial order 8 February 1986, JO 11, published 12 March 1986:** Article 1 expressly replaces four register rows in the annex of the 22 June 1985 order (`F1986011.PDF`, `49cfdbd7aafca943f3c39e33882a24e791ed7531ff251f5b627d9947d111904b`; `A1986011.PDF`, `3d26169e0094ecb115d034fbfc1ab4507910efb8916822b41247a2ca494d3072`). Visually PNG-verified in both languages.
- **Interministerial order 21 March 1998, JO 23, published 19 April 1998:** modifies the 1985 register for the Grand-Alger Governorate and Wilayas 09, 35 and 42 (`F1998023.PDF`, `e618f876aca7d27e7b60e0efe650343942c2b80e9c51e6425d4e223513ef5c8c`; `A1998023.PDF`, `50fb657098ac30e1ddc939483140b94fbb3a96413629898d871452d26d230362`). - **Interministerial order 12 September 2021, JO 79, published 17 October 2021:** modifies and completes the 1985 register (`F2021079.pdf`, `7ca010b9856fea63d4bd8c90cadaa088bbe0e9194d1e0f8edb64e507c57a984e`; `A2021079.pdf`, `288ea0da7013d8c18530a5b03c22a91cb25f40d9fe7ea7c356ecc0fc2b4433da`). Visually PNG-verified across both editions (FR pp. 13–19, AR pp. 15–22).
- **Law 26-06 / JO 25 (2026) and JO 58/2026:** change the legal composition for 21 Wilaya sections and print the 404-row register delta already documented in the current 404-row extract (`F2026058.pdf`, `6a1e59f7c0e8396115657c6376dabeb3fa07543ac8a7036ee3003af6a415aad6`; `A2026058.pdf`, `b1f68aa2794b751b8234a8ff5f9c2dfc1479b996dced2620e9b6f254cf6a889c`, visually PNG-verified across printed pp. 9–25 in both languages). JO58 supersedes prior row assertions for those 21 affected sections, not for the 48 other sections.

**Do not collapse these source families:** amendments to the 1984 territorial-composition framework and seat-setting orders can affect locality composition or seats without themselves establishing a four-digit register-code change. The 1992/1993/2006/2021 territorial-composition instruments in the separate amendment inventory remain distinct from the Decree 85-20 register chain.

### 3.1 Confirmed parent-Wilaya candidate discrepancy: Tabelbala

JO79/2021 French (p. 18) and Arabic (p. 20, `تبلبلة | 5206 | تبلبلة`) both list **Tabelbala** in Wilaya **52** (Béni Abbès), code **5206**, seat **Tabelbala**. The pinned third-party candidate file contains a row labelled Tabelbala, but assigns it to Wilaya **08** (Béchar), candidate key `W08-R012`. It does not have a Tabelbala row in candidate Wilaya 52, which has 9 candidate rows versus the 10 rows declared by JO79. Candidate Wilaya 08 has 12 rows while JO79's Béchar section declares 11. This reciprocal 1-row count difference is consistent with the explicit JO79 source assignment; it is a blocking parent mismatch in the candidate dataset, not a basis for automatic migration. Preserve the candidate row as found, preserve the JO79 assertion 52/5206 separately, and do not change project IDs or source parents without the full temporal lineage, dependent-record impact and practitioner review.

## 5. Historical code conflicts still open

- **Chabet El Ameur:** JO44/1985 visually verified in both French (p. 1069) and Arabic (p. 1634) prints `3514` in Boumerdès; in JO23/1998, visually verified PNG scans prove the Arabic edition (`A1998023.PDF`, p. 13) prints `شعبة العامر | 3514`, whereas the French edition (`F1998023.PDF`, p. 11) prints `Chabet El Ameur | 3614` in the Boumerdès (`35`) table. ONS 2008/2021 geographic references support `35/14 => 3514`, but are corroborating sources, not register amendments. No canonical correction is selected pending practitioner adjudication.
- **Chihani:** Visually verified JO44/1985 PNG scans prove that the French edition (`F1985044.PDF`, p. 1069) literally prints `Chihani | 3624 | Chihani` (duplicating `Raml Souk | 3624`), whereas the Arabic edition (`A1985044.PDF`, p. 1635) literally prints `شهاني | 3614 | شهاني` (and `رمل السوق | 3624`), aligning with ONS 2008 (`شهاني`, 36/14) and ONS 2021 (`شحاني`, 36/14). Preserve both editions' printed values verbatim; no canonical correction is selected without practitioner sign-off.
- **Béni Guécha:** JO44/1985 list and JO11/1986 amendment are separate assertions; JO11/1986 (FR & AR visually PNG-verified) sets seat Béni Guécha (`بني قشة`) for code `3919`; JO79/2021 (FR & AR visually PNG-verified) subsequently lists the commune as `Beni Guecha / بني قشة / 3919`. Candidate text `Ben Guecha` is a source-label variant, not a basis for changing the identity.
