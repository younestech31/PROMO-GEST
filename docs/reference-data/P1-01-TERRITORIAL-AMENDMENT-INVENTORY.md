## 1. Keep two legal source families separate

### A. Territorial register / four-digit commune code and seat

The register chain is anchored by:

- Decree No. 85-20 of 2 February 1985, JO No. 06, 3 February 1985: institutes the territorial register and its code rules.
- Interministerial order of 22 June 1985, JO No. 44, 23 October 1985, printed pp. 1052–1073: initial commune/register-code/seat table.
- Interministerial order of 8 February 1986, JO No. 11, 12 March 1986, p. 280: modifies four commune/code/seat rows.
- Interministerial order of 21 March 1998, JO No. 23, 19 April 1998, pp. 9–11: updates the Grand-Alger Governorate and the Wilayas of Blida, Boumerdès and Tipaza.
- Interministerial order of 12 September 2021, JO No. 79, 17 October 2021: modifies/completes the 1985 register for the 2019 territorial reorganisation.
- Interministerial order of 5 May 2026, JO No. 58, 12 August 2026: modifies/completes the 1985 register with 404 printed commune entries across affected and new Wilayas, while other sections are unchanged references.

## 2. Implications for the 1,541-row concordance

Each row must be reconciled across distinct assertions rather than flattened into one value:

1. **Legal unit identity:** stable internal identity for the commune; do not use the mutable four-digit external code as the only database identity.
2. **Commune name:** verbatim Arabic/French names by source and valid period; include renames such as Aïn Hassainia → Houari Boumediene as source-linked aliases/history, not silent overwrite.
3. **Territorial membership:** commune or locality-to-parent relationship with valid-from/valid-to and the exact Decree 84-365 amendment, Law 84-09 amendment, or other source that changes it.
4. **Register code:** four-digit code as printed by the Decree 85-20 register and each subsequent register amendment; keep old/new code assertions separately where applicable.
5. **Seat:** administrative seat in its own attribute, not a commune name/alias. The 1984 seat order and the register annex are distinct dated assertions.
6. **Evidence status:** distinguish official text extraction, owner-reported visual reading, independent page-image transcription and qualified practitioner adjudication.

A decree that changes a locality's parent, composition or boundary may create a need to reconcile a code or parent relationship, but the code must not be invented from the parent's two-digit prefix. Decree 85-20's code-allocation rules and the applicable register entry must be checked for every transfer. Preserve the source's literal code even when it appears inconsistent; raise an exception rather than auto-correct.

## 3. The 1996 question

The current JORADP search pass did not locate an instrument in 1996 amending Decree 85-20, the 22 June 1985 register order, or Decree 84-365. This is a bounded search result, **not an exhaustive negative legal opinion**.

JO No. 49/1996 includes school-establishment directory annexes that list Chabet El Ameur under code 3514. The underlying decree concerns creation/suppression of schools. This is an official secondary cross-check, not a territorial-register amendment, and its code field must not be treated as the legal commune register without a specific rule establishing that equivalence.

Do not silently replace a project note that says “1996” with “1986”. Ask the reviewer to confirm whether the note meant the 8 February 1986 register amendment or another 1996 instrument.

## 4. Unresolved historical pointer: 13 May 1985 seat amendment

The 2 June 1984 seat-setting order in JO41/1984 is identified. The existence and subject of an order dated 13 May 1985 modifying its commune chief-lieu-seat annex are expressly cited in the 'Vu' clauses of the 8 February 1986 register amendment (JO11/1986, printed p. 280). Its standalone JORADP issue/page and full annex remain unlocated; treat this as an established source pointer with incomplete publication/evidence metadata. Do not infer its full effects from the 1986 recital alone.

## 4C. JO79/2021 — eight re-published existing-Wilaya tables

JO79/2021 prints 103 register rows for eight older Wilaya sections: W01 (16), W07 (27), W08 (11), W11 (5), W30 (8), W33 (4), W39 (22), and W47 (10). The 27 Biskra rows are historical; JO58/2026 later re-composes W07 and creates W61. Four exact French-name pairs move from W07 to W61 with new codes: Branis 0703→6103, El Kantara 0717→6101, Aïn Zaâtout 0718→6102, El Outaya 0719→6104. Djemorah 0720→Djemourah 6105 remains unlinked pending bilingual review. The other 76 rows belong to seven older W sections not modified in JO58/2026 and remain part of current source coverage. See the JO79 existing-Wilaya 103-row extract and transfer-control CSVs.
